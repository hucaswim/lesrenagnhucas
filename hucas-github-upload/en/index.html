ABOUT HUCAS

Learn to swim patiently, step by step, with zero pressure

HUCAS — Everyone Can Swim — is a Bali-based swim school built on the belief that anyone, from age 4 to adulthood, can learn to swim the right way. We focus on building confidence in the water before moving on to technique.

LEVEL SYSTEM

Clear, trackable learning progress

Every HUCAS student progresses through 5 levels, so parents and students always know exactly how far they've come.

1

Water Introduction

Getting familiar with water and building comfort.

2

Basic

Floating, kicking, breathing, and basic coordination.

3

Beginner Swimming

Starting to swim using basic technique.

4

Technique

Refining technique and movement efficiency.

5

Advanced

Further development of technique and swimming ability.

COACH

Guided by an experienced coach

Coach

Arya Singa

Swim Coach · Teaching since 2021

Arya is a former professional swimmer who has been dedicated to the sport since 2010. Drawing from his experience as a competitive athlete, he transitioned into coaching in 2021, with a mission to help more people discover, master, and enjoy the art of swimming.

For Arya, the most rewarding part of being a coach is not simply watching students learn to swim, but seeing the smiles on their faces when they overcome their fear of water and gain confidence in themselves.

Arya believes that everyone should know how to swim. Swimming is more than just a sport; it is an essential life skill that can benefit us throughout our lives.

To him, swimming is a gateway to a whole new world of possibilities and adventures. Once you know how to swim, you can explore the beauty of the ocean through freediving, ride the waves with surfing, experience the thrill of cliff jumping, and enjoy countless other water activities.

"Learning to swim is not just about learning to move in the water. It's about building confidence, overcoming fear, and opening the door to a whole new world of adventures."

— Coach Arya
